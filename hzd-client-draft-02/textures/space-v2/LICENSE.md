## Local material interpretation

The reference is the customer's interior photograph. These maps supply real
surface detail; they do not establish the actual material brand or specification
of the photographed project.

- Wool colour is re-dyed to a low-contrast neutral ground at runtime, retaining
  the scan's fibre normal and roughness maps. Sofa, cushion, throw and rug receive
  different warm neutral material tints and appropriate texel scale.
- A grout-free rectangular crop of one Marble 01 slab is used consistently in
  colour, normal and roughness maps. Modelled floor joints remain independent.
- Roughness ranges are remapped conservatively for the intended honed, matte and
  satin finishes. Source image files remain unchanged.
- Copper/bronze micro-normal and roughness maps are original deterministic
  textures generated by `src/space/materials.js`; they are not third-party assets.
- Standard mesh UVs are converted to metre units. Custom helical UVs and artwork
  canvas UVs are preserved.

All prepared image textures retain `userData.mimeType = "image/jpeg"` to support
portable textured GLB export. Colour maps use sRGB; normal/roughness data use no
colour-space conversion.
